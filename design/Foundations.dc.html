Text on the Righteo button
Every text token clears 4.5:1 on its own surface in both modes, ink-faint included, so placeholders stay readable. There is no red in this system: lateness and incompletion get no colour at all.
The action button is always the inverse of the page: graphite on paper, cream on the board. It is the one deliberately loud object, and it is loud through contrast rather than saturation.

The emphasis ladder
Emphasis is derived from the working set at render time. It is not a status field and must never be persisted as one. This direction spends the highlighter rather than luminance on the top step, so every level carries a cue that is not colour: the ordinal, the label it sits under, or the absence of the mark.
01 Focal
Paper, shadow, and the highlighter on its outcome. One at a time.
02 Active
Same paper, no shadow, no mark. Ordinal stays.
Peripheral
Compact tile under the ALSO ACTIVE label. No border, no ordinal.
Parked
No fill at all. Labelled in words: out of the way, not gone.
Provenance marks
Border style carries the distinction as well as colour, so it survives greyscale and colour blindness. This is the visible form of keeping evidence, narration and inference apart.
narrated
Filled, no border. You said it. Outranks everything below.
observed
Solid border. A collector saw it happen. Not proof it mattered.
inferred
Dashed, unfilled. The model concluded it. Correctable.
Space and radius
Structural: 8 · 14 · 18 · 22 · 30 · 44
Deliberately not a strict 4/8 grid. Section gaps, card padding and gutters use the coarse steps; inside a component, finer values are allowed where optical alignment beats grid purity.
